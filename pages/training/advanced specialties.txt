Advanced Open Water Diver

Expand Your Skills and Dive Deeper

After your Open Water course, the next logical step is to expand your skills and dive deeper into the world of scuba diving with the Advanced Open Water Diver course. This course gives you the opportunity to explore a variety of dive types, expand your dive skills, and gain valuable experience—all in a safe, structured, and fun environment.

Why Take the Advanced Open Water Course?

Go Deeper: With the Advanced Open Water certification, you’ll have the ability to dive to depths of 100 feet/30 meters.

Variety of Dives: You’ll experience new types of dives, including deep diving, navigation, and potentially wreck or drift diving, depending on your location and interests.

Skill Growth: The course allows you to refine skills such as buoyancy control, navigation, and even photography—taking your skills from beginner to advanced in just a few days.

Course Structure: What You’ll Learn

The Advanced Open Water Diver course is designed to help you grow as a diver and improve your overall underwater skills. You’ll complete five dives—two required and three electives—to complete the course:

Required Dives:

Deep Dive: Learn to safely dive to deeper depths and understand the challenges and considerations of deep-water diving.

Underwater Navigation Dive: Improve your underwater navigation skills, including using a compass and natural references to navigate while underwater.

Elective Dives (Choose 3):

Wreck Diver: Explore the mysteries of shipwrecks and practice techniques used to safely navigate and enter wrecks.

Night Dive: Experience the magic of diving at night, where a whole new world comes to life under the glow of your torch.

Search and Recovery Dive: Learn search techniques to locate objects and practice safe methods of lifting and recovering items underwater.

Dry Suit Dive: Perfect for cold-water divers, learn how to dive comfortably and safely in a dry suit, extending your diving season.

Peak Performance Buoyancy Dive: Master buoyancy control and refine your skills, making you a more efficient and confident diver.

Drift Dive: Learn the techniques required to dive with currents, including drift diving skills and safety protocols.

Underwater Photography Dive: Dive into the art of capturing underwater moments, with tips on lighting, composition, and settings for underwater cameras.

Why Choose the Advanced Open Water Course?

Broader Dive Opportunities: With your Advanced Open Water certification, you open the doors to many exciting and new dive sites, plus many dive operators only require this level of certification for certain types of dives.

Confidence Building: This course is a perfect balance of fun and safety, designed to help you gain confidence underwater and push your limits in a controlled environment.

Become a More Skilled Diver: The skills you gain from this course will make you a safer, more efficient diver. Plus, you’ll have a lot more fun while diving!

Specialty Courses Offered

Master New Skills and Unlock New Adventures

Why Choose Specialty Diving?

Learn from Experts: Learn specialized techniques from instructors who are passionate about their fields and bring years of experience.

Specialties to Choose From
Dry Suit Diver

Perfect for cold-water diving, and ideal for divers wanting to stay warm and comfortable
Don’t let cold water keep you from diving. Learn how to use a dry suit to comfortably dive in even the coldest conditions, extending your dive season and expanding your dive locations.

What You’ll Learn:

How to properly wear and maintain a dry suit

Adjusting buoyancy while in a dry suit

How to manage the unique challenges of cold-water diving

Nitrox Diver

Maximize your time underwater with enriched air
Nitrox allows you to extend your bottom time, reduce nitrogen absorption, and dive more safely. If you love longer dives, this specialty is a game-changer.

What You’ll Learn:

Understanding the benefits of diving with enriched air (Nitrox)

Proper handling and analysis of Nitrox mixtures

How to safely plan dives using Nitrox

Wreck Diver

Explore the mystery of sunken ships and hidden history
Shipwrecks hold a treasure trove of history and marine life. As a certified wreck diver, you’ll gain the skills to explore wrecks safely and responsibly, unlocking some of the most awe-inspiring dive sites.

What You’ll Learn:

How to safely navigate wrecks and confined spaces

The importance of proper wreck dive techniques

How to use a reel and line for navigation in wrecks

Underwater Photography

Capture the beauty of the underwater world and share your experiences
Master the art of underwater photography, and learn how to capture breathtaking shots while diving. Whether you want to snap photos for personal memories or for social media, this specialty will help you document your underwater adventures like a pro.

What You’ll Learn:

How to use your camera gear effectively underwater

Techniques for lighting, composition, and exposure

How to work with natural light and artificial lighting while diving

Why Take a Specialty Course?

Build Confidence and Competence: Specialty courses aren’t just about learning new skills—they’re about developing confidence in your diving ability. Each specialty helps you feel more in control and prepared, no matter where you dive.

Gain Access to Exclusive Dive Sites: Certain dive locations require specific training. Wrecks, caves, and deep-water sites often require specialized knowledge, and getting certified will unlock these incredible dive sites for you.

Get Recognized for Your Skills: Each specialty certification is a badge of honor. It shows that you’re a committed and skilled diver who’s passionate about learning and growing in the sport.
