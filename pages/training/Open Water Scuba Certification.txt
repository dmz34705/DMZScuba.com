Open Water Scuba Certification

Train Your Way. Dive With Confidence.

Becoming a certified scuba diver should feel exciting—not rushed, intimidating, or one-size-fits-all. My Open Water program is built around personalized instruction, real-world diving, and flexible options designed to fit your lifestyle, schedule, and budget.

Whether you want a private, one-on-one experience, a small group class with friends or family, or a destination certification vacation, this course is designed around you.

What Makes This Open Water Program Different
✔ Personalized Instruction

No conveyor-belt classes. Training is paced to your comfort level, learning style, and goals as a diver.

✔ Flexible Class Formats

Choose private instruction, small group classes, or family & friend groups—all taught with the same high standard.

✔ Midwest Dive Experience

Train in real Midwest conditions so you graduate confident and capable anywhere you dive.

✔ Payment Flexibility

We offer tiered, pay-as-you-go pricing—ideal for students and young adults who want to start diving without a large upfront cost.
