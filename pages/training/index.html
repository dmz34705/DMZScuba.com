Three Steps to DMZ-Level Diving

Follow the simple path that builds real confidence and capability in the water.

Step 1: Open Water

Earn your OW certification with clear structure, coaching, and solid fundamentals. A strong start to your scuba diving lifestyle.

Step 2: Advanced Open Water

After building confidence, advance by building your skill set and extending your limits. Unlock new dive sites and explore deeper.

Step 3: Specialty Training

This is where your training gets focused. Learn dry suit, Nitrox, or wreck skills to unlock new environments, extend your bottom time, and explore deeper than ever.

Why We Teach SDI

SDI gives us the freedom to tailor each course to the diver in front of us. Instead of rigid, one-size-fits-all progressions, we customize the curriculum to your pace, goals, and schedule.

Current Training Pricing

These rates cover core instruction and certification processing. Site fees, transportation, and personally owned gear stay separate.

Open Water

$1099 per diver for groups of 2 to 4, or $1199 for private Open Water with personalized instruction, plus class materials, pool sessions, dives, pool rental gear, and certification fees included.

Advanced Adventure

$599 with class materials, instruction, required open water dives, and certification fees included.

Rescue Diver

$699 with class materials, instruction, required training sessions, and certification fees included.

Specialties

$399 each for dry suit, Nitrox, wreck, full face mask, and other specialty offerings with required course materials and certification fees.

Skill Refresh

$399 for focused refresher coaching built to rebuild confidence before your next dives.

Not included: open water rental gear, transportation to class, pool, or training sites, mask/fin/snorkel/boots sets, and facility entry fees such as quarry admissions.
