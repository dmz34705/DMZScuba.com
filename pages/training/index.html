How DMZ Scuba Classes Work

You will always know what comes next. Share your goals and availability, and we will build a clear training plan around the way you learn—not ask you to fit yourself into a rigid schedule.

1. Choose a Course

Start with Open Water, try scuba first, refresh before a trip, or move into advanced and specialty training.

2. Request Dates

Share who is taking class, when you are available, where you prefer to train, and what you want to accomplish. We will confirm the right format and timeline.

3. Get in the Water

Complete eLearning when required, then train with steady coaching in the pool and open water.

Fast Start Available

When schedules line up, some students can get in the water in as little as 1 week. Many classes can start within 1 to 2 weeks, while more lead time gives you better scheduling options.

Current Class Pricing

These rates cover core instruction and certification processing. You do not need to pay the full course price just to start the conversation or reserve a plan.

Discover Scuba

$299 per person for SDI Scuba Discovery. If you continue into Open Water within 60 days, that $299 applies toward your Open Water tuition. Open water location fees may vary.

Open Water

$1,099 per diver for groups of 2 to 4, or $1,199 total for private Open Water with personalized instruction. The price includes class materials, pool sessions, certification dives, pool rental gear, and certification fees.

Open Water Referral

$649 per person for SDI eLearning support, classroom review, pool training, referral paperwork, and local scheduling support before destination checkout dives.

Advanced Adventure

$599 with class materials, instruction, required open water dives, and certification fees included.

Rescue Diver

$699 with class materials, instruction, required training sessions, and certification fees included.

Specialties

$399 each for dry suit, Nitrox, wreck, full face mask, and other specialty offerings with required course materials and certification fees.

Skill Refresh

$399 for focused refresher coaching built to rebuild confidence before your next dives.

Not included: open water rental gear, transportation to class, pool, or training sites, mask/fin/snorkel/boots sets, and facility entry fees such as quarry admissions.
