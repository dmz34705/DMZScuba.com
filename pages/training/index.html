Where most divers begin

Open Water turns curiosity into access.

This is the certification that lets a complete beginner become a recreational diver—someone who can join buddy dives, work with dive operators, travel as a diver, and keep building real experience.

  • Learn: equipment, buoyancy, buddy skills, dive planning, and how to respond when something does not go as expected.
  • Unlock: recreational diving within your training and certification limits, DMZ events, travel diving, and continuing education.
  • Choose: group training for 2 to 4 divers, fully private instruction, or a referral path that finishes at your destination.
