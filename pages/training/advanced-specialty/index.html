Why We Teach SDI

SDI gives us the flexibility to tailor advanced training to the diver in front of us. You complete SDI eLearning first, then we use instructor-led dives to focus on the skills that move you forward.

If a skill needs extra reps, we can work it without forcing the rest of the class to rush. The result is a smarter progression, more confidence in real conditions, and a plan built around your goals and schedule.
