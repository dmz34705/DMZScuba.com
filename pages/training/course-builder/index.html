  1. You send the class request.

  3. We confirm dates, pricing, payment options, gear needs, and logistics before anything is final.

Current pricing guide: Discover Scuba pricing is confirmed during scheduling. Open Water is $1099 per diver for groups of 2 to 4, or $1199 for private Open Water. Advanced Adventure is $599, Rescue Diver is $699, Skill Refresh is $399, and specialties are $399 each.

Getting started: The listed price is the complete course package. You can request dates first, discuss a deposit, and choose a payment path before anything is locked in.

After certification: Ask about current DMZ Scuba shirts, merchandise, completion perks, and developing keep-diving rewards for excursions, entry fees, rental fees, and future dive opportunities.

Included pricing covers course materials, instruction, required pool or open water sessions when the course uses them, and certification fees. Transportation, open water rental gear, personal mask/fin/snorkel/boots sets, and facility entry fees stay separate.
