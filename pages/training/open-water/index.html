Scuba Diving International

SDI Open Water is internationally recognized and accepted worldwide. You can take your certification anywhere and dive with confidence—on vacation, on a liveaboard, or with any reputable dive operation. You’re getting the same global recognition as the big-name agencies, with a more modern training approach and significantly better value. At DMZ Scuba, we focus on real results in the water—not inflated pricing. SDI lets us deliver a world-class certification without forcing students into paying for branding over training.

SDI gives us the freedom to custom tailor our training for each diver without locking you into a rigid progression. You complete modern SDI eLearning first, then we use instructor-led time to build real confidence in the water.

That means we can revisit skills you want extra reps on without holding up the rest of the class. It is a calmer, more personal path to certification that keeps your training aligned with your goals and your schedule.

SDI gives us the freedom to tailor training when it benefits the diver, but it also provides a clear, rigid progression for those who prefer it. Some agencies pride themselves on that structured approach, and we get it—many divers learn best that way. With SDI, you can follow the traditional step-by-step program exactly, while still having the flexibility to slow down, revisit skills, or add coaching time when needed.

Swim and Water Comfort Requirements

For safety, every diver must complete the standard swim and water comfort checks before scuba training begins. Scuba requires basic swim ability for obvious safety reasons.

  • Swim 200 yards (or 300 yards with mask, fins, and snorkel).
  • 10-minute tread or survival float.

There is no time limit on the swim. The tread or float confirms comfort and confidence in the water before moving into scuba training.

SDI Junior Open Water depth limits

Age 10-11: 40 ft / 12 m max.

Age 12-14: 60 ft / 18 m max.

Age 15: converts to regular SDI Open Water (normal buddy rules apply).

Supervision requirements (Once Certified)

Ages 10-11 must dive with a parent/guardian or dive professional in direct supervision and close proximity, like a true buddy plus adult escort at all times.

Ages 12-14 must dive with a certified adult diver (18+) who is a parent/guardian or an adult designated by the parent/guardian, and must follow the 60 ft / 18 m limit.
