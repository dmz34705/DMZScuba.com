Why SDI Open Water

SDI Open Water is internationally recognized and accepted by dive operators worldwide. The eLearning-first format gives us more in-person time for the work that matters most: comfort, control, safety, and real skill in the water.

The course follows a clear certification progression while still giving us room to slow down, repeat skills, and coach the diver in front of us.

Swim and Water Comfort Requirements

For safety, every diver must complete the standard swim and water comfort checks before scuba training begins.

  • Swim 200 yards, or 300 yards with mask, fins, and snorkel.
  • Complete a 10-minute tread or survival float.

There is no time limit on the swim. The tread or float confirms comfort and confidence in the water before moving into scuba training.
