How the Referral Path Works

  1. Complete SDI eLearning. Start the academic portion before your pool sessions.
  2. Review and train with DMZ Scuba. Complete classroom review and confined water pool training locally.
  3. Receive referral documentation. We prepare the required referral record for the destination operator.
  4. Finish open water dives while traveling. Your destination dive operator completes the required checkout dives and certification processing.

What You Finish at the Destination

The destination dive operator completes the required open water checkout dives and any destination-specific fees, rental gear, site logistics, and certification processing steps they control.

Referral Pricing

$649 per person for the DMZ Scuba referral portion: SDI eLearning support, classroom review, pool training, referral paperwork, and local scheduling support.

Destination checkout dives, destination operator fees, travel costs, site fees, rental gear, and destination-controlled certification steps are separate.

Important Planning Notes

  • Confirm your destination operator accepts SDI Open Water referrals before travel.
  • Ask about their open water checkout dive schedule, fees, rental gear, and cancellation policies.
  • Leave enough time between local pool training and your trip for paperwork and travel planning.
  • Referral timelines can vary by destination operator, weather, and travel schedule.

Why Use a Referral

A referral path lets you arrive at your destination with the academic and pool work already handled. That can make the trip feel less rushed and let the destination portion focus on open water checkout dives.

It is a good fit when you want local coaching before travel but prefer to finish certification in warm water or at a planned dive destination.
