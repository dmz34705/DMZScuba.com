What You Try

This is not a certification class. It is a guided first scuba experience that introduces the core pieces of diving in a calm, controlled setting.

Gear Assembly and BCD Basics

Learn how the basic scuba system fits together and how buoyancy control starts.

Regulator Use

Practice breathing from scuba equipment with direct coaching and close supervision.

Communication

Use simple underwater signals so you can stay connected and comfortable.

Swim Technique

Try basic movement patterns that help you feel more stable underwater.

Comfort Skills

Build early confidence with simple skills chosen around your comfort level.

Up to 2 Hours on Scuba

Enjoy meaningful in-water time while learning the basics with an instructor nearby.

  • Experience real local diving with direct instructor supervision
  • Location and pricing vary by site

How It Works

  1. Get your eLearning code. Complete the required SDI online learning before your session.
  2. Set your schedule. Once the online portion is complete, we arrange your Discovery session.
  3. Get in the water. Learn the basics and enjoy the experience underwater with direct instructor supervision.

Who This Is For

  • Adults who want to try scuba before committing to certification
  • Teens and families deciding whether Open Water is the right next step
  • Vacation-minded students who want a confidence check before a bigger training plan
  • Anyone curious about scuba who wants a safe, guided first experience

Love It? Keep Going.

After completing the SDI Scuba Discovery program, you earn SDI Scuba Discovery recognition and can continue diving under the supervision of an active SDI Instructor.

If you join the full Open Water course within 60 days, your Discovery experience can qualify you for a discounted Open Water rate. Open water options are available, with location and pricing varying by site.
