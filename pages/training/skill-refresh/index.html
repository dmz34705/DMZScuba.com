Session Formats

Sessions are scheduled around your calendar - weeknights, weekends, or custom blocks - so you can get current without rearranging work or family time.

Pool or Confined Water

Focused practice in calm water for maximum control and feedback.

  • Ideal for long breaks from diving
  • Great for rebuilding basic skills
  • Low stress, high repetition

Pre-Trip Tune-Up

Quick refresh before a travel week or a big dive weekend.

  • Skill confirmation and confidence boost
  • Gear familiarity check
  • Instructor briefing on your destination

Who This Is For

  • Divers returning after a long break
  • Travelers heading on a dive trip soon
  • Anyone who wants to feel calm and confident again
  • Newer divers who want extra coaching

How It Works

  1. Tell us your goals. We pick the best format and schedule.
  2. Train with focus. Calm coaching and repeatable skill blocks.
  3. Leave ready. Confidence, clarity, and a plan for your next dives.
