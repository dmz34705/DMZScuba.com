Why divers specialize

Choose training because a dive calls for it.

Specialties are most useful when they solve a real problem: a colder environment, an enriched-air plan, a wreck goal, or a different equipment configuration.

  • Enter a different environmentBuild the control needed for colder water or structure-focused diving.
  • Make a better planLearn the analysis and limits behind enriched-air use on appropriate profiles.
  • Use specialized equipment responsiblyPractice the setup and emergency procedures before relying on a new configuration underwater.
