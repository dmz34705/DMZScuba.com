Specialty Courses Offered

Specialty training builds confidence and opens up dive sites that require specific skills. Each course focuses on a specific environment or technique. Click a card to learn more about each specialty.

Classes are scheduled as needed around your calendar - weeknights, weekends, or custom blocks - so you do not have to rearrange work or family to train.

Dry Suit Diver

Stay warm, extend your season, and master buoyancy in cold-water conditions.

Learn fit, care, and in-water control.

Nitrox Diver

Maximize bottom time and reduce nitrogen absorption with enriched air.

Plan, analyze, and dive Nitrox safely.

Wreck Diver

Explore historic sites with safe approach, line work, and navigation skills.

Build confidence around structure and access.

Full Face Mask Diver

Stay warm, breathe naturally, and improve comfort in colder conditions.

Ideal for long dives and extra stability.
