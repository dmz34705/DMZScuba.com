Why We Teach SDI

SDI gives us the freedom to tailor dry suit training to each diver. We are not locked into rigid progression, so your training matches your pace, goals, and schedule.

Program Snapshot

Comfort and Warmth

Buoyancy Control

Cold-Water Readiness

What Makes This Dry Suit Program Different

Dry suit training should feel calm, controlled, and confidence-building. We focus on fit, buoyancy control, and real-world cold-water procedures.

Drysuit Fit + Thermal Strategy

Dial in sizing, seals, and layering so you stay warm without bulk.

Buoyancy + Trim Control

Venting, body position, and stable depth changes without surprises.

Safety Procedures

Manage inflator issues, runaway ascents, and cold-water comfort checks.

Midwest-Ready Training

Real conditions so your skills hold up anywhere you dive.

How It Works

  1. Plan. We align on your goals, gear options, and schedule.
  2. Train. Briefing plus in-water drills focused on control and comfort.
  3. Certify. Complete the skills and earn your SDI Drysuit Diver specialty.
