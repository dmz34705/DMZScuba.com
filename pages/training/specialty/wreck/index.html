Where this skill can take you

Historic sites deserve more than an ordinary approach.

Wreck training develops the planning, awareness, positioning, navigation, and line fundamentals used around underwater structures. It supports responsible exploration without implying that every wreck or penetration is appropriate for every diver.

  • Prepare for Great Lakes historyBuild skills relevant to structure-focused local and regional diving when conditions permit.
  • Approach travel wrecks deliberatelyRecognize hazards, plan the dive, and maintain awareness around unfamiliar structure.
  • Protect the site and the diveUse low-impact movement and controlled positioning to avoid contact and preserve visibility.
