Why We Teach SDI

SDI lets us tailor wreck training to each diver. We are not locked into rigid progression, so we can match your pace, goals, and schedule.

Program Snapshot

Wreck training emphasizes safe approach, awareness, and responsible exploration.

Approach and Planning

Understand hazards, entry points, and controlled positioning.

Line Work Basics

Learn reel handling and orientation techniques around structure.

Navigation Control

Maintain awareness and avoid contact with fragile wreck surfaces.

What Makes This Wreck Program Different

Wreck diving demands precision and awareness. We focus on controlled approaches, clean line work, and low-impact technique that protects the site.

Safe Approach Skills

Positioning, stability, and entry planning around structure.

Line Protocols

Reel use, tie-offs, and maintaining orientation.

Low-Impact Movement

Protect the wreck and keep your visibility clear.

Great Lakes Ready

Skills that translate to Midwest wreck sites and travel dives.

How It Works

  1. Plan. We align on the wreck type, conditions, and dive goals.
  2. Train. Briefing, dry practice, and in-water skill progression.
  3. Certify. Complete the skills and earn your SDI Wreck Diver specialty.
